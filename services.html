Services

From early clinical need to validated digital products

01

Product Strategy

Turn an early idea or clinical need into a clear product direction. We define users, workflows, requirements, value proposition, market context and the path to implementation.

02

UX & Product Design

Translate clinical complexity into intuitive digital experiences through journey mapping, information architecture, wireframes, prototypes and human-centered design.

03

Product Build & Integration

Support the move from prototype to working product, including MVP development, healthcare websites, workflow automation, AI-enabled features and digital integrations.

04

User Research & Validation

Test whether the product actually works for the people who need to use it. We support usability studies, interviews, PREMs/PROMs, validation planning and evidence generation.

05

Regulatory & Product Strategy

Integrate regulatory thinking early. Support may include intended purpose definition, MDR strategy, classification considerations, documentation planning and digital-health pathway assessment.

06

Fractional Product Leadership

Hands-on product support for healthcare teams that need someone to connect clinical, technical, design and regulatory workstreams without hiring a full internal product lead.
