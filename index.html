Why DH Product Factory

Healthcare expertise and product development should not happen in silos.

DH Product Factory was created to bridge the gap between healthcare expertise and digital product development. We bring clinical understanding, product strategy, human-centered design, implementation and regulatory thinking together to turn healthcare needs into digital products that can work in practice.

One approach. The right expertise for each project.
