Regulatory & Product Strategy

Digital health classification & validation planning

A digital health product strategy project focused on defining intended purpose, classification considerations, validation needs, evidence requirements and the regulatory path alongside product development.

02 / 03
Location Germany
Type Digital Health Strategy
Focus Classification · Validation · MDR · Product Positioning

The Strategic Question

How should the product be positioned, validated and developed so regulatory requirements support — rather than interrupt — the product strategy?

The work focused on integrating product and regulatory decisions early, rather than treating classification and validation as separate activities after development. This included clarifying intended purpose, understanding classification implications, defining evidence and validation needs and aligning these decisions with product scope and future development.

What Had to Be Defined

Intended Purpose

Clarifying what the product is intended to do, for whom and in which context of use.

Classification

Assessing how product functionality and claims influence medical-device classification considerations.

Validation

Defining how usability, product performance and user-facing functions should be evaluated.

Evidence

Identifying what evidence is needed to support product claims, validation and future regulatory development.

Product Positioning

Ensuring the product strategy, intended purpose and regulatory pathway remain aligned.

Aligning product strategy with regulatory reality

Product and regulatory decisions were worked through together rather than in sequence, so that classification, evidence and validation needs shaped product scope from the start instead of being resolved after the fact.

Current Output

A clearer product direction with regulatory and validation requirements integrated into development planning.

The work provides a structured basis for continued product development, validation activities and regulatory decision-making without separating these considerations from the product strategy itself.
