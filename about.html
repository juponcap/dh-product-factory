Meet the Founder

Juliana Ponton

Physician · Digital Health · Product Development

I work at the intersection of healthcare,
technology and product development.

My work focuses on translating real clinical needs into digital products that are clear, usable and feasible to implement. I combine clinical understanding, product strategy, human-centered design, digital implementation and regulatory thinking to help healthcare teams move from early ideas to products that can work in practice.

DH Product Factory grew from that approach: healthcare products should not be designed in silos. Clinical requirements, user needs, technology, evidence and regulation need to be considered together from the beginning.

The goal is simple: build digital health products that make sense clinically, work for users and can survive beyond prototype.
